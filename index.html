Em-Ployger — Payroll Management System

A payroll system for Philippine employers covering employees, attendance, overtime, leave, pay cycles and payslips. It computes SSS, PhilHealth, Pag-IBIG and BIR withholding tax, generates government reports (BIR 1601-C, Form 2316, 13th month pay), and runs payroll through a Draft → Processed → Approved → Paid workflow with role-based access for employees, HR, the General Manager and administrators. Secured with BCrypt passwords, CSRF protection, login lockout and an audit log.

Highlights

  • Payroll moves from Draft to Processed, is approved by the General Manager, then marked Paid — and every payroll keeps a record of how it was calculated.
  • Paid payroll is never edited. An underpayment or overpayment is recorded and settled in a later payroll.
  • Four roles — Employee, HR Manager, General Manager and Administrator — with the checks done on the server.
  • Payslips print on screen or download as PDF; SSS, PhilHealth and Pag-IBIG tables export as CSV.
  • Automated tests run on an in-memory H2 database, so they never touch the real MariaDB data.

Skye Crafts — Online Shop

An online shop for handmade fuzzy-wire bouquets, keychains and figurines. Customers order for local delivery or pickup, pay by GCash or cash, and track their orders; the owner runs everything from an admin dashboard. Rebuilt from my earlier Flask + SQLite version.

Highlights

  • The cart is kept in the browser, so customers can shop before signing up.
  • GCash orders show the shop’s payment details and the customer uploads a payment screenshot.
  • Stock goes down at checkout and back up when an order is declined, cancelled or expires.
  • Orders left unconfirmed without a receipt are cancelled automatically after 48 hours.
  • Product photos can be taken from the phone camera; they are shrunk and their location data removed before upload.
  • Covered by unit tests, 100 database tests and Playwright browser tests.

Kita Kita — Cash Register for Startups

A cash register web app for small businesses. Owners record sales and expenses, then get insights like net profit, profit margin, cash runway and break-even point, with plain-language tips based on their own numbers.

My role

Front-end developer, UI developer and project coordinator in a team of three.

Highlights

  • A quick register for sales and expenses with amount, description, category and date.
  • Insights for total revenue, expenses, net profit, profit margin and cash on hand.
  • Guidance on runway, gross margin and break-even, plus a warning when cash covers less than three months of expenses.
  • History with CSV export, and settings for the opening balance and currency symbol.
  • Light and dark mode, keyboard navigation and screen-reader labels.
